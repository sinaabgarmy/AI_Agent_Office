# PYSEC-2025-31

> **Layer:** Execution · **CWE:** CWE-841 · **Confidence:** 83%

## Attack primitive
However, in the case when target is an access to a DynArray and the rhs modifies the array, the cached target will evaluate first, and the bounds check will not be re-evaluated during the write portion of the statement.

## Detection
Instrument external calls, state-transition ordering and balance conservation; alert on any reentrant or post-effect external interaction.

## Remediation
workarounds for this vulnerability
